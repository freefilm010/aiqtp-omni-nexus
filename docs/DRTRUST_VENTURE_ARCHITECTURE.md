# DRTRUST / AIQTP Venture Architecture

Status: working strategy document. Not legal, tax, accounting, banking, securities, or investment advice.

## Purpose

AIQTP is not a single app. It is intended to become a staged financial-technology ecosystem owned and managed by DRTRUST (Wyoming): software, data, post-quantum wallet infrastructure, trading research tooling, marketplace rails, and eventually regulated payment/stablecoin and financial-services products where licenses and capitalization support them.

The correct operating model is staged proof, not one-click empire:

1. Build real software people can test.
2. Generate real recurring revenue.
3. Document real intellectual property.
4. Build real compliance controls.
5. Use that evidence to support financing, partnerships, licensing, and valuation.

## Parent-Subsidiary Logic

DRTRUST should hold the core IP and license narrowly defined rights to operating subsidiaries or partners. The point is not artificial circular revenue. The point is clean separation:

- IP risk separated from operating risk.
- Regulated products separated from unregulated software.
- Stablecoin issuance separated from research and trading tools.
- Marketplace fees separated from subscription revenue.
- Future investor stakes can be sold at the layer that matches the investor's mandate.

## What Counts As Real Value

For investor, lender, partner, or regulator review, valuation support should come from evidence, not narrative alone:

- recurring revenue: MRR, ARR, churn, retention, expansion
- usage: verified active users, session frequency, feature adoption
- data assets: proprietary datasets, ingestion pipelines, labels, coverage
- IP: filed patents, trademarks, EIPs, copyrights, code inventory
- technical moat: post-quantum wallet implementation, broker connectors, security controls
- compliance posture: policies, audit logs, RLS, kill switches, incident response
- contracts: customer agreements, partner MOUs, paid pilots, letters of intent
- capital: cash, committed equity, properly structured debt, eligible collateral

Do not rely on fabricated traffic, simulated AUM, circular lease revenue, or no-doc credit as primary proof. Those can damage the credibility of everything else.

## Revenue Hierarchy

Phase 1 revenue should be simple and legal:

1. AIQTP Signals Pro subscriptions.
2. Platform SaaS tiers.
3. Data/API subscriptions.
4. Enterprise research and integration retainers.
5. Marketplace listing/finders fees only after terms and compliance review.
6. Wallet licensing and post-quantum migration services.
7. Regulated products only after licensing and capital readiness.

## Capital Strategy

The venture thesis can support high valuation only if the evidence package becomes strong enough. Capital should be sequenced:

1. Founder/operator bootstrap revenue.
2. Paid beta customers.
3. Strategic angel or small seed check against real metrics.
4. IP-backed or revenue-backed financing only after revenue and clean books.
5. Institutional seed/Series A after traction and compliance review.
6. Regulated-product capitalization after counsel identifies which funds count.

No-doc business lines of credit may help operations if legally obtained and personally risk-managed. They should not be treated as regulatory capital without written professional confirmation.

## 12-Month Target

The first serious milestone is not "be a bank." It is "be impossible to dismiss."

Target evidence by month 12:

- $25k-$100k MRR from subscriptions/data/API/enterprise pilots
- 500-5,000 verified users or waitlist leads
- 50-250 paying customers depending on tier mix
- first EIP or technical standard submission
- trademark filings in progress
- patent provisional(s) filed where defensible
- security cleanup merged
- Stripe subscription flow live
- QWallet MVP demo
- QTC legal memo complete
- investor memo and data room ready

## Non-Negotiables

- No fabricated user, AUM, volume, ROI, APY, charter, or testimonial claims.
- No token sale before securities analysis.
- No stablecoin launch without reserve, redemption, attestation, AML, and licensing plan.
- No live trading route without kill switch, position limits, rate limits, auth, and logs.
- No circular related-party revenue presented as market demand.

## Bottom Line

The DRTRUST architecture is viable as a venture architecture if it is built in proof layers. AIQTP should become a tested, revenue-generating platform first, then use that platform, IP, and evidence to finance the larger regulated ecosystem.
